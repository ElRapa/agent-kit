00design tokens

One look for every tool.Terminal, prompt, pages, widget.

One file, design/tokens.json, builds the Ghostty and herdr themes, the starship prompt, a Gemini CLI theme, CSS for pages and the colours of the edge widget. A project that wants its own accent adds a small variant file instead of a copy.

How to use itPalette

01palette

Warm black, cream ink.Colour only where it means something.

Surfaces are warm greys, not blue ones. Five steps of ink carry most of the hierarchy. The accents name a role: violet is the accent, cyan is info, green ok, amber warn, red error. The three-colour glow is for one thing per screen.

02type

Mono for structure.Sans for reading.

Headings, labels, numbers and anything you could type are JetBrains Mono at weight 800 with tight tracking. A heading's second line is the same face in italic, in muted ink. Body text is the system sans. Labels are small capitals with wide tracking.
